Sustainable Development Goals Division

Leading initiatives across Agriculture, Education, Environment, Health, Digital Inclusion, and Social Development — empowering individuals, institutions, and ecosystems to progress sustainably.

  FSRTI Research Division

Our Vision

To foster sustainable, inclusive, youth-centered, and innovation-driven development aligned with SDGs, empowering communities and institutions to thrive.

Our Mission

To deliver integrated training, digital enablement, research, consultancy, and outreach services that build capacities, promote innovation, and nurture responsible citizenship.

Our Focus Areas

Comprehensive solutions for sustainable development across multiple sectors

Capacity Building, Skill Development & Digital Enablement

  • Training across agriculture, education, environment & health
  • Experiential-learning–based skill development programmes
  • Financial literacy & resource management
  • Digital literacy for youth, communities & institutions
  • Integrated IT programmes ensuring cross-sector convergence
  • Customer Education Programmes (design → delivery → evaluation)
  • Organisational Development (OD) support
  • Curriculum & module development
  • Youth empowerment: leadership, employability, innovation
  • Life-saving skills, resilience training & social preparedness
  • Programmes on social skills, relationships, respect & responsibility
  • Value-based citizenship & law-abiding behaviour

Research, Consultancy & Advisory Services

  • Financial planning, business models, advisory services
  • Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) & feasibility studies
  • Surveys, socio-economic studies & data analysis
  • Social audits, impact assessments & monitoring
  • Government, NGO & institutional consultancy
  • Prototype development for patentable & incubatable solutions

CSR & Outreach Initiatives

  • CSR project design & end-to-end implementation
  • Awareness programmes (agriculture, climate, health, education)
  • Digital empowerment & IT outreach
  • Intern/apprentice training for field & research
  • Rural development & behaviour-change campaigns
  • Family & Child Healthcare programme implementation
  • Youth empowerment & community mobilisation

Academic, Research & Knowledge Contributions

  • Participation in seminars, conferences & academic forums
  • Publishing articles, papers & thematic reports
  • Guiding students in research & field studies
  • Innovations across social, digital, environmental & STEM areas
  • Development of scalable, patentable programme models

Ecosystem Strengthening & Institutional Support

  • Strengthening Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)
  • Enhancing social, cultural, financial, digital & environmental ecosystems
  • Building partnerships across government, academia & private sector
  • Organising cultural & educational tours for experiential learning
